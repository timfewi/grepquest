# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GrepQuest contributors, maintainers, and community members are committed to a
welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the observable impact of behavior.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, consent, and project security boundaries.
- Help make participation accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring anyone to reveal credentials, wallet information, identity, or
  other sensitive data.
- Using project spaces to promote scams, malware, or deceptive financial claims.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone is representing GrepQuest. It also applies to
private project communication when that communication concerns participation
in the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct incidents privately through a contact method published on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private contact method is available, open a
minimal issue requesting private contact without naming people or including
sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities must instead follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review reports as confidentially and promptly as practical,
avoid conflicts of interest, and may request additional context. Proportionate
responses include a private warning, removal of content, temporary participation
restrictions, or a permanent ban. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports are
also violations of this code.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Decisions may be reconsidered when relevant new information becomes
available.
